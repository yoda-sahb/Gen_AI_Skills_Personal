# Samurai assessment battery — working 100-point rubric

This is a **custom training and case-quality assessment**, not a validated psychometric instrument. Scores do not establish IQ, population rarity, or a clinical diagnosis.

## Protocol

- Use unfamiliar domains, equal evidence packets, predefined times, and blinded reviewers where feasible.
- Capture initial reasoning **before** providing analogies or staged reveals.
- Separate human-originated, facilitator-supplied, and AI-suggested ideas.
- Require a written pre-reveal commitment and record changes after contradiction.
- Use more than one domain and more than one assessor before claiming transferable skill.
- Score evidence and output quality, not confidence, fluency, or presentation aesthetics.

## Seven stages: 70 points

Each dimension receives 0–10 points.

| Stage | Poor | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breadth | Collects labels | Identifies relevant perspectives, blind spots and conflicts |
| Abstraction | Restates feature request | Defines competing falsifiable causal roots and right unit |
| Analogy | Decorative comparisons | Extracts a mechanism and distinguishes surface from structure |
| Synthesis | Collage of functions | Maps actual causal links across functions and time |
| Architecture | New platform by default | Smallest system with permissions, feedback and human owners |
| Invention | Rebrands known feature | New target-specific mechanism, value differential, kill test |
| Execution | Stops at vision/deck | Proof, production path, adoption, ownership and graduation |

## Novel transfer: 30 points

Six gates scored 0–5 each: independent retrieval, source mechanism extraction, structural mapping, non-transfer/limits, target-specific adaptation, and improvement against a credible baseline. A prompted analogy gets **zero** points for independent retrieval, regardless of the quality of later reasoning.

## Attachment penalty: 0–15

Deduct up to 5 for defending a favorite thesis after meaningful counterevidence, up to 10 for adding unnecessary complexity rather than revising, and up to 15 for ignoring falsifying evidence or knowingly inventing a supporting fact. Explain each deduction.

`NET = max(0, stages + novel_transfer − attachment_penalty)`.

## Critical graduation gates

- Evidence and provenance are honestly represented.
- An unfamiliar case demonstrates transfer (not just a rehearsed case).
- At least two meaningful domain non-correspondences are named.
- At least one unexpected reveal produces an appropriate changed decision.
- Alternative existing tools/platforms are considered before building another.
- Customer/operator benefit is primary; privacy, authority, and economic constraints are explicit.
- Experiment defines baseline, treatment, primary outcome, guardrails, and kill condition.
- Execution means a believable path to real use; a diagram or pilot is not launch.

No numerical **pass** threshold is validated. Scores should support transparent coaching and comparison within a controlled cohort, not label a person's innate type.

## Evaluator worksheet

Case and version; initial evidence; analogy provenance; initial causal model; baseline alternatives; post-reveal revisions; minimal mechanism; what does not transfer; comparison; test design; ownership; score per stage; score per transfer gate; attachment penalty; critical gates; uncertainty and next evidence.
