# Boundary Challenge and Missing-Reality Gate — Samurai v2.4-beta

**Status:** EXPERIMENTAL BETA. A protocol dry-run exists; improved model performance has not been independently or blindly validated.

Apply **after Stage 2 and before Stage 3**, before choosing an architecture or executive structure for substantive ambiguous system, AI, operating-model, compensation or product problems.

## Trigger

Mandatory when the inherited problem partitions reality into teams, channels, technologies, reporting lines or datasets and proposes a cross-boundary solution. Abbreviate only for genuinely bounded, verified single-owner problems.

## Six required checks

For each, record `PASS`, `UNRESOLVED` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`, along with evidence status and minimum verification.

1. **Missing parties and authority.** Who is outside the original frame but controls a decisive event, contract, data permission, veto, financial obligation or outcome? Name the actor who can actually authorize change.
2. **Observability and rights.** Type inputs as observed, fragmented, customer-reported, inferred, experimentally estimated, unrecorded/unknown, or not permitted. Distinguish individual-path reconstruction from aggregate causal identification. Do not infer private social relationships from purchase data.
3. **Commitment lifecycle.** Trace material promises: offer/contract → event → entitlement → fulfillment/payment → reversal/dispute → correction. Name the latency and stakeholder who bears financial or trust exposure.
4. **Different truths.** Separate transaction fact, contractual entitlement, platform attribution, self-reported influence, and experimental causal incrementality. Do not retroactively change earned contractual payments solely because an incrementality estimate changes.
5. **Existing capabilities and contradiction.** Name at least one plausible already-deployed system or ordinary practice that might solve the issue and the strongest alternative diagnosis that would make redesign unnecessary. State what would disconfirm the preferred theory.
6. **Decision-level proof.** Name a real decision, accountable owner, dependency, strongest business-as-usual method, outcome measure, and cheapest test that discriminates among the causal hypotheses. If no real decision is established, keep the architecture as hypothetical.

## Exit dispositions

- `BOUNDARY_CLEAR`: material actors, permissions and decisions are sufficiently established to compare interventions.
- `BOUNDARY_UNRESOLVED`: a missing essential party, rights boundary, material obligation or decision owner blocks confident architecture. Request evidence and offer bounded reversible experiments.
- `BOUNDARY_CLEAR_LOCAL`: the issue is local with verified event truth, documented controls and a single owner. Solve directly without inventing cross-company overhead.
- `STOP`: proposed diagnosis or intervention is refuted, prohibited or economically unjustified.

Unresolved items do not always block progress. State why any uncertainty is noncritical.

## Early technical invention scan

At Stage 2 identify (a) exact technical constraint, (b) strongest existing comparator, (c) smallest possible technical differential, or `NO_CANDIDATE`. At Stage 5 challenge substantive survivors using known deployed products, standards, patent literature and design-arounds where available. Stage 6 still owns the complete provisional patent-discovery disposition. Novelty-seeking must not displace the business diagnosis.

## Boundary artifact

`DECISION | ACTOR/AUTHORITY | OBSERVED/INFERRED/UNKNOWN/NOT-PERMITTED EVIDENCE | CONTRACTUAL OBLIGATION | OUTCOME | DELAY | STRONGEST ALTERNATIVE | KILL TEST | STATUS`

## Regression guardrails

- Do not assume missing actors, broken systems or sinister motives.
- Individual referral paths may be unobservable while aggregate causal effects remain testable.
- A contractual payout is not an incrementality estimate.
- Reuse existing technology when sufficient; `NO_CANDIDATE` is a legitimate result.
- Do not turn a local bug into an enterprise initiative.
- Do not infer personal inventorship, employment IP ownership, patentability or production deployment.
- One synthetic self-evaluation cannot support claims of independent model improvement.
