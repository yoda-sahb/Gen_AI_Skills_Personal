# Novel Transfer — Evaluate a mechanism, not a metaphor

Novel transfer is the selective adaptation of a causal mechanism from one domain to improve a different decision or system. A surprising analogy is not enough.

## Six gates

1. **Independent retrieval.** Record `SPONTANEOUS`, `PROMPTED`, or `UNKNOWN`. If a facilitator, AI, supplied brief, or reading supplied the analogy, do not claim spontaneous retrieval.
2. **Mechanism extraction.** Identify source actors, state, inputs, decisions, constraints, actions, outcomes, feedback, and plausible causal explanation.
3. **Structural mapping.** Which *relationships* genuinely correspond in the target? Why?
4. **Non-transfer and limits.** What material assumptions do not carry across? Identify at least two important non-correspondences and a stop condition.
5. **Selective recombination.** Adapt the reusable kernel without importing source-specific baggage, governance, or incentives.
6. **Decision improvement.** Compare to a credible baseline that does not use the transfer. Does the transfer improve the explanation, evidence, value, test, or actual outcome?

The response must allow `NO_TRANSFER`, `ANALOGY_ONLY`, `TRANSFER_UNPROVEN`, and `TRANSFER_PLAUSIBLE`. Only actual comparison evidence warrants `TRANSFER_TESTED`. A mechanism may be locally inventive without being novel in patent law.

## Example: outside an observable system

A fictional household-product company cannot observe some private referrals and offline purchases. It may have messy internal data **and** events no internal system records. Cleaning the data handles the first; connecting data sources can handle some of the second; neither reconstructs never-recorded private conversations.

**Transfer candidate:** a control/measurement-system principle: when the state needed for a decision is not identifiable from available signals, change the observation or experimental design rather than simply applying more computation to those signals.

**Where it transfers:** distinguish observed from inferred state, introduce voluntary new product interactions for future customer-value signals, and use well-designed randomized experiments or aggregate data when individual paths cannot be observed.

**Where it does not:** this cannot recover the actual past referral chain, guarantee identity matching, justify monitoring private communications, or make consented participants representative of all households. Observability and *causal* identification are different questions.

**Baseline:** good data hygiene plus existing customer profiles and measurement tools.

**Adaptation:** two explicitly distinct loops: (1) permissioned, customer-useful context/action/outcome memory, (2) independent causal incrementality testing where an aggregate outcome is measurable. Typed evidence distinguishes observed, self-reported, estimated, and unknown.

**Status:** conceptual transfer only; not first-in-world or validated business impact.

## Negative transfer

A joint-task-force model can suggest temporary cross-functional assignments and bounded mission ownership, but not an authoritarian command structure in a civilian organization. Import the coordination mechanism only if incentives, employment arrangements, and decision authority make it appropriate.

A trainee gets no score merely for saying the two domains are similar.
