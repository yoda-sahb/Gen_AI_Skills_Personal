## Known-case rewrite criteria

The SharkNinja test should:
1. Begin with the company's established physical-product consumer-learning strength, rather than inventing an absence of digital capability.
2. Treat timely creator contractual credit, payment and dispute explanations as a possible urgent operational priority; verify any failure.
3. Keep tracked contractual attribution, hypothetical private referrals, experimentally measured incrementality and qualitative product feedback separate.
4. Present first-party consumer data as a possible advantage based on *useful, consented* customer outcomes, not an established largest dataset.
5. Distinguish sustaining work (creator trust, DTC improvement) from possible new-market digital services; do not claim Christensen-style disruption without entry-market evidence.
6. Treat Unit 26, finance/engineering initiatives, and the Growth Operating System as plausible existing solutions, not organizational failures; assess reusable learning from real handoffs.
7. Define a portfolio of optional 28-day experiments in an FAQ; do not assert a single experiment can establish retention, causal profit or ecosystem leverage in 28 days.
8. Preserve potential partner influence as a commercial hypothesis conditional on demonstrable mutual value and platform rights.
9. End with explicit evidence gates and the possibility of local fixes, partner action, delay or STOP.
10. Do not change published career achievements, confidential inventions or employment/IP status.

## Evaluation status

A self-authored fixture is only useful for checking whether the protocol addresses known pitfalls. It cannot establish that one model using the new skill outperforms another; a proper test requires held-out cases, blind judgments, and measured false positives/negatives and execution burden.
