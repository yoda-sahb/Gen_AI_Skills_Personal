# Executive Whitepaper Writer v1.0

**Clear argument. Accurate evidence. Natural prose.**

A standalone Agent Skill for writing cohesive executive white papers, strategy papers and board-level analyses from research or working notes.

The failure it addresses is not lack of ideas. It is the tendency of AI writing to transform a substantive argument into disconnected headings, impressive-sounding terms and unsupported certainty.

## What it does

- Establishes one governing thesis and orders supporting claims by logical dependence.
- Separates documented facts, user reports, inference, hypotheses and recommendations.
- Builds the argument through continuous paragraphs, with exhibits only when needed.
- Removes "I think," unsupported certainty, promotional slogans and unnecessary consulting jargon.
- Runs argument, evidence, language and document-cohesion edits before delivery.
- Preserves the original source meaning, financial metric classifications, confidentiality and IP limitations.

It is independent of Harvard Business Review, BCG and McKinsey; no affiliation or endorsement is claimed.

## Try it

\`\`\`text
Use Executive Whitepaper Writer to turn these notes into a cohesive
executive paper. Preserve the evidence and uncertainty, use neutral
third-person language, avoid consulting jargon and overuse of
headings and bullets, and develop one continuous argument.

[PASTE NOTES AND SOURCES]
\`\`\`

## How it fits the existing skills

**Samurai** / **Root-to-One CEO Brief**: discover the correct problem and test the business thesis.  
**Executive Whitepaper Writer**: turn the findings into a readable, substantiated, decision-ready paper.  
**Storytelling**: use only when an explanatory story or narrative reveal is required.  
**Wordsmith**: refine sentence-level language without changing the facts or argument.

This skill does not invent new evidence, bypass confidentiality review or replace upstream research.
