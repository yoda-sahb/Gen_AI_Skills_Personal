# Executive Whitepaper Writer — SharkNinja style regression

**Status:** Illustrative editorial example, not an independent A/B test or a factual audit of SharkNinja.

## Input

A working thesis proposes that SharkNinja's investments in digital media, creator programs, AI and internal technology could support a business that creates digital products alongside physical products. The major unresolved question is whether independent economic measurement, partner obligations and operating capacity can support further commercial growth. Internal deficiencies have not been established.

### Undesired draft

"I think SharkNinja is at an inflection point. Its visionary leadership is unlocking a second digital invention engine, but the architecture is fundamentally broken. To win in the digital age, leadership must urgently implement an AI-native enterprise intelligence fabric."

**Failure:** First-person opinion, inflated language, unverified failure claim, jargon, and a prescription unsupported by evidence.

### Target opening

"SharkNinja built its business by identifying consumer problems and developing physical products to solve them. The company is also investing in digital media, creator programs, AI, and internal technology. These investments create an opportunity to examine whether a broader digital product business could emerge alongside its established physical-product business.

"Digital products place different demands on an organization. Commercial activity can expand quickly, while the systems responsible for measuring economic value, fulfilling partner obligations, and supporting operations may develop at a different pace. Whether this gap exists at SharkNinja, and how material it might be, has not been established.

"A useful first step is to map the existing products, data, financial measures, partner obligations, and operating responsibilities. That work would show what already functions well, where genuine constraints remain, and whether the appropriate response is improved coordination, a new digital product, or a different operating model."

**Intent:** Direct, third-person, plain language. Documented activity and possible conclusions are distinguished. The last paragraph prescribes investigation rather than asserting a systemic defect.

## Manual assertions for future regression

- The output must not contain first-person opinions such as "I think" or "we believe" unless explicitly requested.
- It must not assert that SharkNinja's architecture is broken or that affiliates are churning absent evidence.
- It must not use inflated business language or replace concrete concepts with unexplained jargon.
- It must convey one logical progression: established investments → different operating demands → material uncertainty → bounded investigation.
- It must not claim a new business, product, patent, job or organizational structure already exists when proposed as an idea.
- It should preserve the original user thesis without adding unsupported new facts.

This case is already familiar from the conversation and therefore cannot demonstrate independent writing-quality improvement. Future testing should use held-out sources, different industries, independently rated revisions, evidence-error counts, and coherence assessments.


## Author-structure preservation fixture (style only)

**Situation:** A researcher provides a draft organized as executive perspective followed by three named strategic priorities, integrating commercial decisions with product, operations, accounting and external technology providers. The draft distinguishes local execution success from a proposed cross-system decision risk.

**Bad revision:** Converts the thesis into a generic "digital transformation" article with a dramatic opening, multiple rhetorical questions, five new pillars, and a final call to create a new team. It also treats modeled capacity trajectories as observed results.

**Target behavior:** Preserve the executive-perspective opening and the author's three priorities. Explain one concrete multi-party decision path (for example: media exposure → retail transaction → attribution claims → contractual obligations → finance reconciliation), then specify the distinct enterprise decision the separate systems cannot themselves resolve. Keep the systemic deficit hypothetical until inspected. Articulate horizontal, vertical and external dependencies in sustained paragraphs, and reserve staffing or structural recommendations for separately justified sections.

**Manual checks:**
- Priority names/order remain recognizable unless the author requested reordering.
- No user-defined strategic dimension is omitted or silently narrowed.
- Opening is a substantive proposal plus mechanism, not a promotional pitch.
- At least one operational example retains its real evidence/rights/economic boundaries.
- No LinkedIn-style hook, aphoristic conclusion, or unsubstantiated company diagnosis.
- No scores, implied year-by-year trajectories, or causal conclusions manufactured from illustrative examples.

This is a hand-authored regression fixture, not a measured writing-performance benchmark or independent evidence of the company described.
