# Samurai Skill: Attribution Stress-Test — 2026-10-16

**Status:** `INTERNAL_METHOD_STRESS_TEST / SIMULATED`, **not** independent/blind model validation, human psychometric testing, real company experimentation, invention verification, or commercial proof.

## Research question

Can the Samurai chain `breadth → abstraction → analogy → synthesis → architecture → invention → execution` prevent an apparently reasonable data-wrangling solution from becoming an oversized and causally mistaken architecture? What smaller invention remains after adversarial evidence?

## Method and limitations

- One assistant used the published skill and its scoring criteria to reason through successive fictional case revelations.
- The initial conventional solution was reconstructed as a comparator, **not produced by a separately blinded baseline model**.
- No actual customer, product, marketing, or financial experiment was run.
- Historical conversation already supplied the observability analogy, so its retrieval must be classified `PROMPTED`; spontaneous novel-transfer ability is **not established**.
- Public literature provides a prior-art check: customer profiles/identity resolution, agent-assisted service, and advertising conversion-lift experiments already exist. Their generic mechanisms are **not inventions here**.
- The synthetic calculation is reproducible in `attribution_simulation.py` and illustrates a logical risk, not expected real-world magnitude.

## Resulting candidate mechanism

### Boundary-Aware Outcome Loop

**Two different unknowns need two different learning loops:**

1. **Context-to-outcome loop:** A customer receives real help; with explicit scope-specific permission, the product records a useful goal/context → chosen action → customer feedback/outcome record. Use existing customer identity, profile, and agent infrastructure. The value is better subsequent help, not corporate data extraction.
2. **Causal lift loop:** Use randomized exposure or well-designed geographic experiments paired with reliable aggregate outcomes to measure campaign incrementality *without* pretending to know each private social referral chain. If measurement is infeasible, do not claim causal lift.
3. **Epistemic contract:** Every signal and conclusion retains its grade: `DIRECT_OBSERVATION`, `CUSTOMER_REPORTED`, `INFERRED`, `EXPERIMENTAL_ESTIMATE`, `UNKNOWN`, or `NOT_PERMITTED`. Agents and executives should not silently upgrade one type into another.

This is a **working composition hypothesis**. Individual elements and many combinations have industry antecedents. Global novelty/patentability is not established. The purpose is to connect two distinct decision questions and prevent one system from impersonating the other.

## Synthetic numeric counterexample

The deterministic script models 100,000 fictional households, including unrecorded social referrals, correlated click propensity and biased voluntary participation. The planted randomized campaign impact is **+1.5 percentage points**.

Expected result with the fixed seed:

- Estimated treatment-control lift: **+1.345 percentage points** (sampling variation).
- Observable click-attributed purchasers: **1,136**.
- Estimated incremental purchasers in the treated population: **670**.
- Opt-in participation: **27.83%**.
- Opt-in conversion rate: **13.04%** versus **8.27%** overall.

**Interpretation:** last-touch counts, randomized incremental effects and opt-in profile samples answer different questions. The count difference does not estimate a real campaign's bias. The simulation is constructed; it is not empirical proof the candidate invention works.

## Cheapest decisive follow-up tests

- **Existing-substrate audit:** Are the relevant goal/action/outcome/permission records and decision APIs already present? If yes, stop the new component.
- **Narrow customer-value pilot:** Randomize access to a permissioned, goal-aware help journey versus existing help. Measure task success, return usage, user trust and explicit permission without making data capture the customer goal.
- **Marketing experiment feasibility:** Confirm a credible retailer/aggregate outcome and valid treatment/control before proposing lift. If unavailable, say measurement cannot support the claim.
- **Selection bias audit:** Compare consenting and nonconsenting populations using lawful, nonidentifying aggregate metrics where feasible.
- **Owner test:** Can one mission leader drive a bounded cross-functional build without requiring a wholesale reorganization?

## What the test changed in the skill

- Observability and **causal identifiability** must be tested separately.
- A permissioned opt-in data product requires a representativeness warning.
- Existing capability may reduce invention to a record/contract or eliminate it entirely.
- Refusing to invent when the incremental gap is empty is a successful outcome.
- A polished analogy must never receive spontaneous-retrieval credit.
