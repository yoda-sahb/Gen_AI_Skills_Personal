STRATEGY SKILL

Product Value-Strategy

ChatGPT Claude
Find where the buyer actually has a reason to choose you.

Use it when something looks relevant or differentiated, but you still need to know whether it should win.

01

What matters?

Start with the decision-maker and the outcomes that actually change the choice.

02

What can they do instead?

Compare against the incumbent, internal build, current team, another vendor, multiple specialists, delay, or reallocation.

03

Where are you really better?

Separate relevance from scarcity, proof, and actual advantage over the strongest alternative.

04

What will people actually do?

Put incentives, budgets, authority, politics, and resource reallocation back into the model.

BUILT-IN STRESS TEST

The Behavioral Reality Check

Efficient for the system does not mean rational for the actor. A strategy is not finished if it only works because someone stops spending, gives up economics, cooperates against their incentives, or lets a saved resource disappear.

Put the people back in the model.

EXECUTIVE STRATEGY SKILL

Root-to-One CEO Brief

ChatGPT Claude
Make the decision clearer before you make the solution bigger.

Use it when a hard strategic problem may be sitting at the wrong system layer and the executive team needs a reframe, evidence plan, and decision.

01

Unlock

Start with the business outcome and the invariant that cannot be compromised.

02

Root + Reframe

Separate the visible symptom from the underlying constraint and find the right system layer.

03

Prove

Require a useful visual, a documented real example, and the cheapest decisive experiment before architecture expands.

04

Build to One

Only after the thesis earns it, define what must become real, launched, repeatable, and ready to graduate into scale.

STORY SKILL

Storytelling

ChatGPT Claude
Make the audience understand the idea before you name it.

Use it when the idea is strong but the story is flat, over-explained, framework-first, or hijacked by a good anecdote.

01

Lock the thesis

Decide what the story is actually revealing before the anecdote, example, or framework takes over.

02

Create a live question

Open with an observation, decision, contradiction, tension, or puzzle that makes the next beat necessary.

03

Show before name

Make the mechanism intuitive first. Introduce the framework or term only after the audience already understands it.

04

Earn the ending

Close by changing how the opening is understood, not by attaching a generic moral, question, or call to action.

Articles Posts Talks Executive narratives Personal stories Original ideas

THE RULE

A strong story does not announce the insight. It makes the audience see why the insight has to be true.

Storytelling shapes the narrative first. Wordsmith can then tighten the language without changing the thought.

WRITING SKILL

Wordsmith

ChatGPT Claude
Make the sentence better without changing the thought.

Use it when the idea is already there but the wording is loose, generic, over-polished, corporate, or simply not memorable enough.

01

Lock the meaning

Preserve the claim, facts, uncertainty, and important caveats before touching the words.

02

Cut before replacing

Remove filler, repetition, throat-clearing, and jargon before inventing more language.

03

Keep it concrete

Prefer strong nouns, verbs, and language that is easy to understand and say aloud.

04

Protect the voice

Do not professionalize away distinctive phrasing just because a smoother generic sentence exists.
